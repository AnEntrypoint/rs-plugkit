# AGENTS.md

Module-scoped rules for the orchestrator sources in this directory. The [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) owns the ownership map and the delivery rules.

## Disciplines, fibers and calculus

- `capability_proxy::resolve` and `discipline_note::requires_satisfied` must match providers identically: both use `resolve_key_realm` (an empty or self-named realm maps to the discipline realm), require an Active provider and check `provides`.
- `discipline_note::active_policies` is the only caller of discipline `advance_fiber`, once per `instruction`. `all_known_discipline_dirs` includes disabled names with state files, so a disabled discipline advances to Inactive.
- `build_interception_context` folds enabled-file order with right-biased `MergeKind::combine`; for `ScalarOverwrite` the last nonempty declaration wins. `handle_check_removal` is the only writer of `enabled.txt` and CAS-writes against the snapshot it read.
- Guest `fiber_lifecycle::transition` is mirrored arm for arm by agentplug-host `registry.rs` `PluginFiberLifecycle`; the guest has only Inactive, Active and Unloading.
- `calculus::verify_calculus` and `formal/CordisCalculus/` change together; it stops silently at `max_states`, so `ok` covers only explored states. Base-registry unload's `retired || !satisfied` equals the paper's `target != omega`; ExtendedRegistry has no retirement flag.
- `component_loader_dispatch::parse_entries` silently drops entries that fail to deserialize; `isolate` is the tagged `{"kind": "none"|"local"|"global"}` shape; `claim_audit_clean` passes only on the exact body `clean`.

## Dream-RSI replay

- Replay is frozen-world evidence, not execution or deployment authority: records stay bound to their completed dispatch and the owner session.
- `observe_dispatch` (`dream_rsi.rs`) appends one JSON line per real dispatch to `.gm/dream-rsi/<session>/observations.json` through `pkfs::append` (no read-modify-write, so concurrent dispatches cannot drop each other's line); past 256 records the whole live file moves to a write-once `observations-archive-<ts>.json` via a compare-and-swap truncate (newest 32 kept; a legacy JSON array is archived the same way), and `session_history` reads archives plus the live file, with `dispatch_id`, `verb`, `fingerprint`, `exit_code`, `gate_drift`, `prd_open_count`, `mutable_open_count`, `quality`, `ts`, `reply_sha256` and optional `lean_node`, `error_code` and `no_match` (both omitted when absent or false, defaulted on read). `failure_class` sets them only for a failing dispatch: `error_code` is the first token-like (`[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]`, at most 64 chars) `error_code`, `error_kind`, `reason` or `code` at the reply's top level or under `data`; `no_match` means no such code and a zero `match_count`, `total_matches` or `returned`, so the empty answer scores quality 0.5 and is not counted in `failure_rate`. Orchestration verbs and `dream-*` dispatches are not recorded; `prd-add` and `prd-resolve` are, so a hop can cite its filed row.
- No rs-plugkit path writes `active-strategy.json`; `active_strategy` only reads it, and writing it is a deployment that rides the normal gated paths. Replay results persist as proposals instead: `dream-replay` and `dream-round` write `.gm/dream-rsi/<sid>/replay-result.json` (`kind`, `source`, `owner_session_id`, `ts`, `baseline_policy_id`, `selected_policy_id`, `baseline_score`, `selected_score`, `improved`, `never_worse`, `beta1`, `beta2`, `rankings[{policy_id, score, cost}]`); a `replayed` `dream-replay-cycle` writes `.gm/dream-rsi/<sid>/cycle-result.json` (`history_round`: `selection`, `incumbent_policy_id`, `selected_policy_id`, `selected_policy`, `baseline_score`, `selected_score`, `improved`, `never_worse`, `candidates`, `rankings`, `beta1`, `beta2`, `history`, `contract`, plus `cycle_id`, `last_dispatch_id`). Both replies carry `replay_result_path`.
- Score each world as best attained quality minus `beta1 * summed cost` plus `beta2 * parallelism_bonus`; a policy scores the mean over its worlds, and a challenger is promoted only when it is scored on at least `PROMOTION_MIN_WORLDS` (3) worlds and its mean score exceeds the incumbent's by `PROMOTION_MARGIN` (0.02) on the same worlds; otherwise the incumbent is kept. Betas are explicit finite nonnegative inputs (cycle defaults `DEFAULT_CYCLE_BETA1`/`DEFAULT_CYCLE_BETA2`).
- `evaluate_policy` expands `group_size` nodes per wave (rounds = waves when set), `stop: converged` ends after a wave that does not raise quality, `stop: exhausted` ignores `max_nodes`, and `attempt` charges a node scoring below 1 `attempt` times its cost (a frozen world replays the same result).
- `history_round` is the project-wide cycle: every session under `.gm/dream-rsi/` is one world (`start` root, one node per verb, mean quality, dispatch count as cost, observed transitions as children). An observation counts only when the dispatch ledger or a `.gm/exec-spool/out` reply carrying its `dispatch_id` confirms it; the rest are counted in `history.dropped` by reason. `dream_candidates` dreams variants of the incumbent (`active-strategy.json` `policy` rooted at `start`, else `pi0-default`); precondition: incumbent among candidates; postcondition: selected score at least the baseline, else the incumbent is kept and `contract.defects` names it.
- `dispatch_ledger::record` compare-and-swaps the ledger (`host_cas_write`, 16 attempts) so concurrent dispatches do not drop entries.
- `dream-replay-round` is session-owned progressive reveal from the policy roots and the revealed frontier; it closes at `max_rounds` or `max_nodes`, and `max_online_rounds` bounds new distinct values.
- `dream-replay-cycle` is observation-only maintenance: it uses the canonical owner `session_id`, replays the whole project history through `history_round`, defers when no new confirmed dispatch exists project-wide, and never refreshes phase clocks.
- Dispatch admission is not gated by Dream-RSI: no `admit_dispatch`, no `dream_rsi_advisory` field, and `instruction` writes no `.gm/dream-rsi/*/reorientation-ts` marker. `.gm/last-instruction-ts` is stamped by `gates` only for a prose-serving dispatch.
